Yong-Woo Lee / 이용우 / 李鎔宇

Welcome. I am a PhD student in the Department of Mathematical Sciences at Seoul National University.

My research focuses on probability theory and mathematical physics, with a specific emphasis on Random Matrix Theory (RMT), including the integrable structures and spectral properties of Ginibre ensembles, Wishart matrices, and Coulomb gases.
